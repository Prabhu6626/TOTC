Frequently asked questions

Honest answers to common questions about how we work, what things cost, and what to expect.

Questions and Answers

It depends entirely on the scope. A focused application with a few core modules for a small team will cost significantly less than an enterprise system serving hundreds of users across multiple locations. We don't publish standard pricing because every project is different. After our discovery phase, we provide a detailed proposal with a clear price—no hidden fees, no surprises. We're happy to discuss ballpark ranges for your specific situation in an initial conversation.

A focused project (single department, core workflows) typically takes 3-6 months from discovery to deployment. Larger enterprise projects with multiple departments, complex integrations, and data migration can take 6-12 months or longer. We give you a realistic timeline after the discovery and requirements phase—we'd rather set honest expectations than promise a deadline we can't meet.

Manufacturing and logistics are our core specializations—it's where we have the deepest experience. But we serve any organization that needs reliable, custom IT solutions. We work with healthcare providers, financial institutions, retailers, energy companies, educational institutions, and professional services firms. The common thread is businesses that need technology tailored to their specific operations.

We don't build and leave. After deployment, we transition into a managed support arrangement where we monitor your systems, apply security patches, manage backups, and respond to issues according to defined severity levels. We also proactively identify improvements and help evolve the system as your business grows. This is a separate, ongoing service with its own agreement and clear scope.

Yes. We regularly integrate with existing ERP systems, accounting software, legacy databases, third-party APIs, and other tools our clients already use. We can also take over support and maintenance of existing custom applications built by other providers. During discovery, we assess your current technology landscape and plan integrations carefully.

Requirements often evolve as you see the system taking shape. We handle this through a formal change request process. If you want to add or modify something, we document the change, assess the impact on timeline and cost, and give you a clear quote before proceeding. No work happens on changes without your approval. For time-and-materials engagements, this flexibility is built into the model.

Data migration is a dedicated stage in our process. We extract data from your existing systems (spreadsheets, databases, paper records, legacy software), clean and validate it, and load it into the new system. We test the migration thoroughly before going live, and when needed, we run old and new systems in parallel during the transition. We never rush data migration—bad data in a new system is worse than bad data in an old one.

Ownership terms are clearly defined in each project agreement. For custom-built applications, you typically own the code that's specific to your business. Some projects may use our proprietary frameworks or libraries under license. We're transparent about this before the project starts. You'll always have access to your data, and we'll never hold your own systems hostage.

Security is built into every system from the start, not bolted on afterward. We implement role-based access control, multi-factor authentication, data encryption (in transit and at rest), comprehensive logging, regular backup testing, and documented incident response procedures. The specific security measures depend on your industry and compliance requirements. See our Security & Reliability page for full details.

Absolutely. In fact, we often recommend it. Starting with a focused project for one department or one process lets you see results quickly, validate the approach, and build internal confidence before expanding. We design all systems with scalability in mind, so adding modules, users, or integrations later doesn't require starting over.

Managed support is a defined service—not an unlimited promise. It includes 24/7 system monitoring, scheduled security patching, automated backups with restore testing, incident response according to agreed severity levels, and monthly reporting. The exact scope and response targets are documented in your service agreement. Anything outside the agreed scope is quoted separately. See our Managed IT & Support page for full details.

Start with a conversation. Contact us to schedule a free, no-obligation consultation. We'll listen to your situation, ask questions to understand your needs, and tell you honestly whether and how we can help. If there's a fit, we'll propose a discovery engagement to map your requirements in detail. If there's not a fit, we'll tell you that too.
