IT support as a defined service

Support is a defined service with agreed response targets, not an unlimited promise. We're honest about what's included, how fast we'll respond, and what you can expect.

Coverage

What our managed support covers

A comprehensive suite of services that keeps your systems running, secure, and up to date.

24/7 Monitoring

Continuous automated monitoring of your servers, applications, and networks. We detect issues before your users do and respond according to defined severity levels.

Support Hours

Business-hours help desk for standard requests. Extended and 24/7 support available as add-ons. All response times are documented in your service agreement.

Security Patching

Scheduled security patches and software updates applied during agreed maintenance windows. Critical patches applied as emergency changes with your approval.

Backups & Recovery

Automated daily backups with regular restore testing. We don't just back up your data—we verify we can actually recover it. Offsite copies for disaster recovery.

Monthly Reporting

Clear monthly reports showing uptime, incidents, patches applied, backup status, and recommendations. You'll always know the health of your systems.

Proactive Improvements

We don't just maintain—we improve. Regular reviews identify performance optimizations, security hardening opportunities, and feature enhancements.

Response Targets

Severity levels and response targets

Every incident is classified by severity. Response and resolution targets are defined in your service agreement.

Severity Description Response Target Resolution Target
Critical System down, all users affected, business operations stopped 15 minutes 4 hours
High Major feature unavailable, significant user impact 30 minutes 8 hours
Medium Feature degraded, workaround available, moderate impact 2 hours 24 hours
Low Minor issue, minimal impact, enhancement requests 8 hours 5 business days

Note: These are standard response targets. Actual targets are agreed individually with each client based on their needs and support tier. Response targets indicate when we acknowledge and begin working on the issue, not when it will be fully resolved.

Our Honest Approach

Support done right

We believe in being straightforward about what managed IT support means. It's a defined service with clear boundaries, not a vague promise that we'll fix anything at any time. Here's what that means in practice:

  • Scope is documented. Your service agreement lists exactly what's included. No ambiguity.
  • Response targets are realistic. We set targets we can actually meet, not aspirational numbers.
  • Out-of-scope work is quoted separately. If you need something beyond the agreement, we'll quote it clearly before starting.
  • Reporting is transparent. Monthly reports show exactly what happened, what we did, and the health of your systems.
