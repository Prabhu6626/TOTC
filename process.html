Eight stages from concept to continuous improvement

A structured, transparent process that keeps you in control at every step. You'll always know where your project stands, what comes next, and what you'll receive at the end of each stage.

01

Discovery

We learn how your business operates today. This involves on-site or remote workshops, stakeholder interviews, workflow observation, and a review of your current tools and processes. We need to understand your world before we can improve it.

Output: Discovery report with process maps and pain point analysis
02

Requirements

We document exactly what the system needs to do, in plain language you can review and approve. This includes functional requirements (what it does), non-functional requirements (how fast, how secure), and user stories for every role in your organization.

Output: Requirements specification document, signed off by your team
03

Proposal & Agreement

A clear proposal with scope, timeline, milestones, and cost. We explain what's included, what's not, and how we'll handle changes if your needs evolve. No fine print, no surprises.

Output: Signed project agreement with clear scope and pricing
04

Design & Planning

We design the system architecture, user experience, database structure, and integration points. You'll see wireframes and prototypes before we build. We also create a detailed project plan with milestones and review points.

Output: Architecture document, UX wireframes, project plan
05

Build & Test

We develop the system in iterative cycles, delivering working features regularly for your review. Every component is tested—unit tests, integration tests, and user acceptance testing with your team. You see progress throughout, not just at the end.

Output: Working, tested application ready for data migration
06

Data Migration

We extract your data from legacy systems, spreadsheets, and paper records, clean and validate it, and load it into the new system. We test the migration thoroughly before going live, and we run parallel systems during the transition when needed.

Output: Clean, validated data in the new system with audit trail
07

Deployment & Training

We deploy the system to production, train your entire team, and provide intensive on-site or remote support during the first weeks. We create user manuals and video guides, and we run train-the-trainer sessions so your own staff can onboard new users.

Output: Live system, trained users, documentation package
08

Support & Improvement

The launch is the beginning, not the end. We provide ongoing monitoring, maintenance, patching, and support with defined response targets. We proactively identify improvements and help you evolve the system as your business grows.

Output: Ongoing managed support with monthly reporting
